2. The predict_material() Pipeline Architecture

Implemented in src/predict_new_material.py, the function performs 5 automated steps:

1 Physical Boundary & Sanity Validation

Rejects negative masses and zero curing durations. Checks total wet density ($pprox 2,200 ext{ to }2,500 ext{ kg/m}^3$) and warns if the curing age exceeds the 365-day training boundary.

2 Exact Schema Formatting

Converts input variables into a 1-row pandas DataFrame matching the exact feature names and data types used during model training.

3 Champion Model Inference

Loads models/final_material_model.joblib (our 92.0% $R^2$ Gradient Boosting model) and generates the continuous prediction in Megapascals (MPa).

4 Materials Engineering Indicators & Grade Classification

Computes key chemistry ratios and categorizes the predicted strength into standard structural civil engineering grades:

  • < 20 MPa: Lean Concrete (Sub-bases, non-structural paving, mass filling).
  • 20 – 35 MPa: Standard Structural Concrete (Residential foundations, slabs, columns).
  • 35 – 50 MPa: Heavy Commercial Structural Concrete (Multi-story buildings, highway bridges).
  • > 50 MPa: High-Performance Concrete (Pre-stressed beams, skyscrapers, marine structures).

5 Mandatory Scientific Disclaimer

Explicitly prints that the output is an algorithmic statistical estimate, not a physical destructive test, preserving engineering safety standards.
